# 90-second offline judge demonstration

Run the app on an iPhone Simulator or signed iPhone. Before presenting, rehearse on that hardware; simulation/batch duration varies. No API keys, login or network are required.

1. **0–15 seconds — Living city.** Open Crisis Lab → START LIVE DEMO. Return to City: the deterministic seed-2026 economy is at day seven. Orbit/pinch and identify a bank. Explain that 1,000 citizens and 80 businesses are posting real transactions.
2. **15–35 — Financial disaster.** Crisis Lab → Trigger The Great Bank Run. The app computes 28 days from the saved baseline. Open City, enable Banking stress, inspect a bank or its recent payment disruptions. Unmet withdrawal attempts are not money destroyed.
3. **35–45 — Rewind.** Crisis Lab → Rewind World B. World A retains the recorded crisis future; World B restores the actual day-seven checkpoint. Ask: “Could a policy have changed this?”
4. **45–60 — Intervention.** Apply liquidity & replay. World B receives an explicitly issued, repayable central-bank advance equal to half the targeted bank's baseline deposit liabilities at a fixed contractual rate. Both futures use the identical crisis and controlled exogenous draws.
5. **60–75 — Compare.** At the matching final day, switch World A/B and read the comparison. Discuss actual employment, business survival, stress, unmet attempts and the public claim. Do not describe added liquidity as money saved.
6. **75–85 — Human impact.** Open Citizens, inspect the same person across worlds and show actual employment, balances, stress and recorded transactions. Outcomes vary by exposure; do not claim every citizen benefits.
7. **85–90 — Explain.** AI Economist → Explain the impact of the bank run. The deterministic offline explanation quotes calculated flows and recorded consequences.

Restart with Restart live demo. Custom crises and policies have editable previews and require confirmation. To cancel a preview, tap Cancel; no event is scheduled. The educational model is not a real-bank forecast. Optional model inference and TestFlight are not required or claimed.
