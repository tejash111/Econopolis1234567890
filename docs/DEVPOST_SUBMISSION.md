# ECONOPOLIS — A Living Financial Digital Twin

## Inspiration

Financial crises are networks of decisions and obligations. ECONOPOLIS turns those connections into an explorable miniature city, allowing a presenter to compare a crisis future with a funded policy response.

## What it does

An offline iPhone simulation creates 1,000 fictional citizens, 80 businesses, four banks and six districts. Users watch real ledger transfers, trigger typed economic shocks, restore a pre-crisis snapshot and compare independently calculated futures. The offline economist explains verified metrics and opens validated command previews. A fictional citizen can join a new shared baseline.

## How it works

Swift 6 actor isolation protects deterministic economic state. Integer-cent accounting records transfers, rejected attempts, public credit and explicit money issuance. RealityKit renders procedural institutions and grouped population activity, with native SwiftUI navigation, Charts and SwiftData persistence. iOS 26 Liquid Glass has an iOS 18 material fallback.

## Distinctive demo

The Great Bank Run preset computes a crisis from a day-seven baseline, preserves World A, restores World B and replays with repayable central-bank liquidity. Comparison distinguishes cash-flow disruption, public claims and actual economic outcomes. There are no hardcoded rescue results or cloud dependencies.

## Limits and next steps

This is a synthetic educational mechanism model, not a forecast of actual banks, markets or policy. Fully backed custody banking uses a documented accessible-settlement constraint. Physical-device performance/accessibility profiling and signed distribution require further verification. Optional Foundation Models inference is not integrated. See the repository test report for actual executed verification; do not claim TestFlight distribution.

## Built with

Swift 6, SwiftUI, RealityKit, Swift Charts, SwiftData, Foundation, structured concurrency, XCTest and Swift Testing. No third-party assets, external backend or paid API.
