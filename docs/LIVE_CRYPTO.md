# Tokenized stocks — CoinGecko Demo

This optional online reference replaces Binance stocks. The fictional economy, crisis demo and replay remain fully offline. No external prices enter the financial ledger or economic metrics.

## Connect

1. Open City for the compact horizontally scrollable stock strip or Economy for all six inline rows. The default company xStock listings are SpaceX, NVIDIA, Apple, Tesla, Microsoft and Amazon.
2. If no key is saved, choose **Connect**; otherwise **Market options → Market connection** opens secure setup.
3. Paste a Demo key and choose **Save key and connect**. Credentials stay in the device Keychain.
4. Use the stock-strip scroll gesture to see additional companies on City. Economy shows prices and seven-day mini charts directly, with no market navigation destination.

Featured IDs are resolved from the actual catalog by company name and xStock listing identity, rather than guessed symbols. Missing companies never produce fabricated rows. Generic crypto tokens matching the company name are excluded. The six listings were verified via authenticated market requests on 2026-10-24. UI labels omit provider branding at the user's request; this document retains provenance/configuration.
